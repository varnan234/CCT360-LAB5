# Sounds of Hyderabad

Lab 5 for CCT360 (Design with Sound & Vision).

Live site: https://varnan234.github.io/CCT360-LAB5/

## Part A: Soundscape

`audio/hyderabad-soundscape.mp3` is 1:28 long and goes through one day in Hyderabad:

- 0:00 Fajr - the morning azaan over a quiet street
- 0:14 Temple - temple bells while the street wakes up
- 0:27 Charminar - traffic, honking, an auto pulling away, people talking in Telugu
- 0:50 Marfa - marfa drums with a crowd
- 1:04 Isha - the night azaan, then it fades out

I layered the clips, faded them into each other and evened out the volumes, then exported it as an mp3.

I couldn't find a marfa recording I was allowed to use, so I made the marfa rhythm myself (marfa drum, daffs and small cymbals). It's my own version, not a recording of a real band.

## Part B: Webpage

- The soundscape uses an HTML5 audio player with controls, plus my own Play/Pause, Volume and Start over buttons
- While it plays, the picture, title, text and page background change to match each part of the day (using timeupdate and if/else)
- The picture fades between scenes and slowly zooms in while the sound is playing
- The skip buttons jump to each part, and the current one is highlighted
- The cards at the bottom play single sounds and shake while they play
