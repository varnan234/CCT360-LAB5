# Sounds of Hyderabad

CCT360 Lab 5. A soundscape of my hometown Hyderabad and a webpage that changes with it.

Live site: https://varnan234.github.io/CCT360-LAB5/

The soundscape (audio/hyderabad-soundscape.mp3, 1:28) goes through one day: morning azaan, temple bells, Charminar, a wedding with marfa, then the night azaan. The picture and background change for each part, and the cards at the bottom play the sounds on their own.

I couldn't find a free marfa recording so I made the marfa beat myself.
